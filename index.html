NASA SPACE MISSION DESIGN GAME // ARCHITECTURE MANUAL

1. The 4 Mission Segments & Failures

  • Launch Segment: Rocket failure = immediate explosion, huge budget loss.
  • Space Segment: Sensor failure = zero data collected, complete waste.
  • Ground Segment: Comms failure = zero data received on Earth.
  • User Segment: Data corrupted = zero market profit.

2. NASA Data Processing Levels

L0 (Raw) → L1A (Annotated) → L1B (Calibrated) → L1C (Inferred) → L2 (Simulated) → L2A/B → L3 (Stitched Maps) → L3A (Temporal Stack) → L4 (Multi-sensor synthesis = $$$ endgame!).

3. Market Buyers

Scientific Institute: Standard price, +Rep.
Open Source: Free ($0), massive ++Rep.
Shady Government: Huge cash ($$$), but --Reputation!

4. The 4 Crew Roles

Launch Player: Rocket design & mechanical repairs.
Space Player: Instruments & sensor calibration.
Ground Player: Computer & comms tracking.
User Player: Data refinery, finances & market deals.
